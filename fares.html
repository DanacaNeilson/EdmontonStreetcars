Up to five people

Family fare covers up to five people, with a maximum of two adults aged 18+ and up to three children under 18. Day cares, preschools, and field trips are not eligible for this rate.

How it works

Board first. Pay your conductor.

Payment options

Cash, debit, credit, and mobile payments are accepted.

Advance sales

Tickets and seats cannot be purchased or reserved in advance. Boarding is first come, first served.

Stop-overs

Stop-overs are welcome. Your return ride may be taken at any scheduled time during the season.
