Our History

1908

A city begins to move

Edmonton’s electric streetcar system began service, connecting growing neighbourhoods and reshaping how residents experienced the city.

1951

The original era ends

After more than four decades of service, Edmonton’s original streetcar network made its final run as buses became the city’s primary transit mode.

1980

Preservation takes hold

The Edmonton Radial Railway Society was formed by volunteers committed to restoring historic cars and keeping Edmonton’s streetcar story alive.
