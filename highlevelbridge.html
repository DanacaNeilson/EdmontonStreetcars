Plan Your Ride

Edmonton’s signature streetcar experience

High Level Bridge

Ride former Canadian Pacific Railway track from historic Whyte Avenue, through Alberta’s only tunnel built on a CPR mainline, and across the river into downtown.

Season

May 15–Oct 12, 2026

Journey

Whyte Avenue to downtown

Tickets

Purchased from the conductor

The route

Four stops. Countless views.

Stop over along the route and use the return portion of your round-trip ticket on any scheduled departure, even on another operating date.

01

Whyte Avenue

Begin near the heart of Old Strathcona and the farmers’ market.

02

Strathcona Barn & Museum

Visit the society’s streetcar museum and see where the fleet is cared for.

03

Government Centre

Step off near the Legislature grounds and river valley trails.

04

Jasper Plaza

Arrive downtown less than a block from Jasper Avenue.

When to ride

Regular service runs Thursday through Monday until Labour Day, then Friday through Sunday through Thanksgiving Monday.

Where to park

Paid parking options are available near Strathcona, Whyte Avenue, Government Centre, and Jasper Plaza stops.

Before boarding

Service is first come, first served. Streetcar availability can change due to maintenance and operations.
